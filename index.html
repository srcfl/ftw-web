01 / The energy core

One system.
Your choice of interface.

Your meter, solar, battery and charger each report their own readings. FTW reads them together, plans ahead and controls on the equipment's own network. The core runs on your hardware and keeps working when an app or assistant disconnects.

MEASURE

See what is happening.

Live power, stored energy and device health. Fresh readings, estimates and missing data stay distinct.

PLAN

Work towards your goals.

Energyplan combines prices, solar, household forecasts and charging needs into one local plan.

CONTROL

Check the result.

Core checks commands against site and equipment limits, then checks the readings for the response.

02 / An energy primitive for AI

Your home energy.
In the conversation.

Ask why the battery is charging. Understand tonight's plan. Get help connecting your equipment. FTW gives an authorized assistant the context to answer.

The FTW plugin brings that access to ChatGPT. MCP gives agents a structured way to work with the core. You choose who gets access and can revoke it. Core checks every request and keeps control at home.

04 / Planning that stays local

The whole home.
Planned together.

Prices change. The car plugs in. The forecast changes. FTW updates the plan around your goals and the energy available.

  • Charge the car towards the level and time you set.
  • Plan battery use around prices, solar and household needs.
  • Coordinate supported equipment from different brands.

Energyplan runs locally. Core checks each proposed plan before it reaches a device. Site limits and fresh meter readings govern control.

Your hardware. Your core.

Install FTW on Linux ↗

Raspberry Pi OS, Debian and Ubuntu

Already running FTW? Follow the switch or update guide. The retired 2.x and 3.x lines receive no more updates.

Moving an older installation?

Keep a full backup and your old data. On a Raspberry Pi, keep the old SD card and use a second card. Guided transfer of old settings and history is not ready. Stop old Core and its autostart before another Core controls the same equipment. If an install refuses the old layout, follow the guide before trying another path.
